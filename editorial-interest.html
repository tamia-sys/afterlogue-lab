Afterlogue / Research / 05

Interest, before prediction.

Can a film's independently reviewed cinematic qualities give Afterlogue a useful hint about a person's interest? This research form keeps the hypothetical prediction hidden until you have entered your own interest choices.

Entirely offline. Nothing is sent to Afterlogue or saved automatically. Both reviewers' private note files are processed in your browser memory. The only export is an aggregate summary without film titles, individual interest choices, reviewer identities or notes. This is a research instrument—not the Home recommendation engine.

01 — Load two independent film reviews

Ask two different reviewers to complete the original film-notes pilot independently. Import their JSON files below. Only matching observations from two reviewers count.

No reviewer notes loaded.

02 — Your own viewing perspective

This part is for someone who did not author either review. Select one film you've watched and liked, and another you've watched but disliked. For the remaining films you haven't watched, say whether you're drawn to watching them. This measures interest, not enjoyment after watching.

03 — Before seeing any prediction

Only answer for films you have not watched. Confirm “I have not watched this film” for each title before choosing a response; otherwise it will be omitted. “Unsure” is valid. Unreviewed titles remain in the list so missing evidence produces an honest abstention. No synopsis or film-style label is shown to influence your choice.

04 — Aggregate-only research result

This is not accuracy, precision or proof of viewer preference. The study is tiny, self-selected and based on subjective labels. A mismatch does not mean someone is wrong; abstention means the evidence isn't sufficient.
