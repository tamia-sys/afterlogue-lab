Afterlogue / Research only / 04

Cinematic evidence pilot.

A small optional 12-film slate for independently authored notes about storytelling and cinematic form. Instead of treating TMDB tags as film criticism, this experiment records what a reviewer has actually observed.

Offline by design. This page makes no catalog, account or analytics requests. Your notes stay in this browser's temporary memory until you choose to export a JSON file. They are not saved automatically, sent to Afterlogue, or used to change recommendations. Only note films you have genuinely watched.

01 — Original viewing observations

The film list contains titles only—no pre-filled opinions or inferred pacing. Unknown dimensions are allowed. Aim for two or three thoughtfully supported observations per film rather than filling every field.

02 — Compare independent reviewers

Have a second reviewer complete their own notes before they see another reviewer's file. Then import their separate JSON exports here. This is a suggested blind workflow, not a technically enforced blind study.

Agreement only means two people chose the same descriptive labels. It does not prove the label is objectively correct, or that someone will enjoy the film. An exploratory chance-corrected statistic is shown only with enough overlapping, varied responses from exactly two reviewers. Disagreements remain unresolved; no majority label is forced.

03 — Test interest, not just agreement

When two reviewers have independently described the same films, a separate offline study can hide the resulting interest hypothesis until a third person records their own choices about films they haven't watched. This is a research instrument, not an accuracy claim or production recommendation.

What happens after the pilot?

We compare reviewer consistency and missing information first. Only then could we investigate whether descriptions of genuinely unfamiliar films help predict a viewer's voluntary interest—under appropriate licensing and privacy safeguards. Nothing here changes Afterlogue's actual recommendations.
